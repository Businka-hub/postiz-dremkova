The tool is not open to the public. It is used by the operator to plan and publish posts for her own brands and for clients who have asked her to manage their social media and who connect their accounts themselves.

What data we process

When a Facebook Page or Instagram professional account is connected, we receive and store:

  • the account or Page ID, name, username and profile picture;
  • the access token issued by Meta, used only to act on that account;
  • the content we schedule and publish (text, images, videos) and its publishing status;
  • post and account statistics (reach, impressions, engagement) and comments on published posts, shown to the operator inside the tool.

We do not collect data about the followers of connected accounts beyond what Meta returns with comments and aggregated statistics, and we do not use any data for advertising or profiling.

Why we process it

Only to publish content on the connected accounts on the date chosen by the account owner, to show the result of publishing and statistics, and to reply to comments when the account owner asks for it. Legal basis: performance of a contract with the account owner (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Where it is stored and who sees it

Data is stored on a server in Prague, Czech Republic (EU), administered by the operator. Access tokens are never shared. Data is not sold or passed to third parties; it is sent only to Meta (Facebook, Instagram) when a post is published on the account owner’s behalf.

How long we keep it

As long as the account stays connected. After the account is disconnected or a deletion request is received, the access token, account data and scheduled posts are deleted within 30 days. Nightly backups are overwritten after 14 days.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, object to processing, or withdraw access at any time in your Facebook or Instagram settings. See Data Deletion. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).
